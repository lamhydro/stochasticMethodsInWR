\section{Part I: Introduction to probability and statistics  }
\begin{enumerate}

    \item \label{s11} From the \emph{IDEAM}, \emph{CAR}, \emph{IDIGER} or other national or regional agencies, obtain a \emph{time series (TS)} of \emph{hourly precipitation} in $mm$ in any place in Colombia.  The series must have at least 15 yrs of continuous hourly records. 

    \item \label{s12} From the hourly $TS$, build a series of the following data: 1)  duration (hours) of rainfall events, 2) intensity (mm of rainfall) of the continuous event and 3) time (hours) between rainfall events. 

\item \label{s13} Accumulate the hourly $TS$ to \emph{daily}, \emph{monthly} and \emph{annual} precipitation.

\item Estimate the \emph{mean}, the \emph{variance}, the \emph{skewness}, the \emph{kurtosis} and the \emph{interquartile range} for the original $TS$ and the $TS$ build in \ref{s12} and \ref{s13}.  Organize the results in tables and perform the analysis of the results.

\item For each of the $TS$ in \ref{s12} and \ref{s13}, make the following plots: the \emph{series}, the \emph{histogram} and \emph{cumulative histogram}. Analyze and discuss the results.

\item For each of the series in \ref{s12} and \ref{s13}: estimate the \emph{pdf}  and the \emph{cdf} using at least two different methods (e.g. \emph{method of moments} and \emph{method of maximum likelihood}). Apply \emph{goodness-of-fit tests} to choose the best \emph{pdf}/ \emph{cdf} that describe the data; made $Q-Q$ plots.  Plot the chosen \emph{pdf}s and \emph{cdf}s and  organize the distribution parameters in tables. Analyse and discuss the results. 

\item Estimate the \emph{confidence intervals} for the \emph{mean} and for the \emph{variance} of the $TS$ in \ref{s12} and \ref{s13}.  

\item Estimate the join \emph{pdf} between the \emph{precipitation intensity} and \emph{duration} and plot it. Estimate the \emph{correlation} between both variables. Analyze and discuss the results.

\end{enumerate}
