\section*{Time series analysis}

For the following hydrological time series:
\begin{itemize}
    \item annual average streamflow,
    \item monthly average streamflow, and
    \item monthly precipitation,
\end{itemize}

whose length must be at least \emph{20 years}, acquired from \emph{IDEAM}, \emph{CAR}, \emph{IDIGER}, or other national or regional agencies, do the following:

\begin{enumerate}

\item Plot the \emph{time series}, the \emph{histogram}, and the \emph{cumulative histogram}. Analyze and discuss the results.

\item Estimate the \emph{mean}, \emph{variance}, \emph{skewness}, \emph{kurtosis}, and \emph{interquartile range}. Organize the results in tables and analyze them.

\item Determine the presence of a \emph{trend} in the series. Check whether the trend is statistically significant using, for example, the \emph{Mann–Kendall test}. Discuss the results.

\item Compute the \emph{spectral density function} or \emph{periodogram} of the series. Identify the main harmonics and fit a sinusoidal model if periodicity affects the stationarity of the series. Discuss the results.

\item If this is the case, remove the trend and/or periodicity from the time series and ensure that the series is \emph{stationary}. Plot the treated series.

\item Compute and plot the \emph{autocorrelation function (ACF)} and the \emph{partial autocorrelation function (PACF)} with confidence limits. Analyze their shapes and make inferences about the possible model type and order.

\item Fit a \emph{Box–Jenkins ARIMA} model to the residual time series. Report the model order and parameters, \emph{AIC} and \emph{BIC} criteria and discuss the results.

\item Plot \emph{model residuals} and test residuals for \emph{normality} and \emph{randomness}. Discuss the results. 

\item Reproduce the original series using the fitted model and plot the results.

\item Use the model to forecast the time series 10 units ahead and plot the results withe confidence intervals for 75\% and 90\%.

\end{enumerate}
